// 03 Reclaiming the Path Entry 03 +

In my early years I would participate in a myriad of different academic challenges and accelerations much to my dislike and discomfort, but I think becoming uncomfortable is the only true way to understand something. Yet, I hadn't figured out what I needed to understand.

Many times when I had my head down in a text book I would much rather be in a fantasy land pertaining to the newest show that I had watched that evening prior. Although cumbersome I found outlets through activities such as the arts and reading. I remember how much I used to read as a kid. Reading tales of far away lands today sound about as much as fiction as the stories themselves.

Later my grandmother actually showed me the world wide web, and the lustrous world of video games. Her and I would often indulge ourselves in point-and-click mysteries. A childhood saturated with adventure often found through a story or a click — I think that is what I learned to understand, or so I thought.

The overbearing guilt and responsibility of growing up in a traditional society where one is supposed to graduate from an institution, get a salary job, and have a family is just cliché. I think what I came to understand is how lonely I am for following a path that I never really decided fully for myself. I believe it is time to reclaim the future, my dreams, my ambition. What it means to be the architect of my own creation, and not destruction.

// 02 On Drums, China Poet, and April Entry 02 +

I had been experimenting with Imagiro and their whole line up of VST's since February. After my release of Alabaster I wanted to revisit making something more abridged, and decided to make an April project that would be my thesis on paper.

Writing piano first has been a staple in my sound design. I usually process ping-pong delay alongside a secondary granularized version of the initial piano sketch that I write to give a nice feeling of enclosure with these melodies.

The drums are always a struggle for me most of the time so I decided to hit up one of my producer friends for some advice. That's when I found the page China Poet on Instagram. The emphasis on melodrama is just the tip of the iceberg. Selectiveness is something that is obsessive when I hear this producer's sounds. Everything is crafted to support their sound, but enough so that each component has its own story, its own vitality, its own "something special".

I bought both of his kits, and I appreciate the "Game Like" presence. Similar to a game that builds stories and character with texture and selectiveness — isolation joined together in harmony. Alone in a room full of people. I am currently working on a new edition to my discography which will be a new drum kit called the "coloring book".

// 01 Self Foundation Entry 01 +

What is a foundation? The literal diction is the lowest load-bearing part of a building, typically below ground level. But what happens when you put "self" in front of the word? Well — I believe you change its meaning entirely.

Putting yourself first is something I never thought of while making my music or doing anything artistically, but it is something I think more of now as I grow older. I always thought of my modalities as something that would be for the betterment of others, but I would soon be met with burnout, and disharmony from within myself.

I recently have been making shifts in my life, and I have realized that I wanted to be an educator. This way I can enrich those around me whilst still building my own self foundation. The literal diction of self foundation is "a strong and positive sense of self to be a foundation for immunization against depression, but it is hard to build and maintain".

Any job of construction is hard to do alone, so I had to find builders with similar ideas of building a place void of oppressive and hedonistic prerogatives. These builders — for that I am grateful. They are the ones who help me lift pillars too heavy. Who know when it's time to rest, and when it's time to work. A community who puts themselves first but enriches others. A place where one can truly smile.
